# Inner-city-modular-train-course-build-

## P.A.C.E. Curriculum Module: Urban Rail Edge Infrastructure (UREI-101)

### Program Overview
A year-long collaborative technical project where students design, fabricate, and assemble the outer shell, structural chassis, and mechanical running gear for a bidirectional, low-profile inner-city data center rail pod.

### Core Philosophy
Students focus entirely on the structural shell, transit ergonomics, mechanical integration, and weatherproofing, while drop-in electronics and power cassettes arrive pre-engineered.

## Phase 1: Mechanical & Spatial Design (Fall Semester - Q1)
- **Rail Geometry & Track Tolerances:** Study standard track gauge, clearance profiles, and how an urban rail pod navigates tight city-radius turns compared to road vehicles.
- **Computer-Aided Design (CAD):** Draft the bidirectional shell framework with symmetrical dual-cab layouts and design the central floor-pan belly housing for a heavy battery pack used as ballast stability.
- **Deliverable:** Complete 3D digital blueprints and structural stress-simulation reports for the pod’s outer frame.

## Phase 2: Structural Fabrication & Material Handling (Fall Semester - Q2)
- **Material Selection:** Work with lightweight aluminum and steel composites to keep weight down while maintaining structural rigidity.
- **Welding & Chassis Assembly:** Fabricate the main longitudinal members, cross-members, and secure mounting points for wheel bogies and the central floor pan.
- **Deliverable:** A fully welded or assembled structural chassis frame ready for mechanical integration.

## Phase 3: Wheel-Rail Interface & Suspension (Spring Semester - Q3)
- **Bogies & Running Gear:** Mount custom or modified low-profile wheel sets and spring-damping suspension systems designed to handle weathered municipal rail surfaces.
- **Braking & Guidance Integration:** Install mechanical brake linkages and guidance alignment components onto the student-built frame.
- **Deliverable:** A rolling chassis verified via load-bearing bench tests and mechanical inspection.

## Phase 4: Shell Integration, Weatherproofing & Final Assembly (Spring Semester - Q4)
- **Exterior Shell & Aerodynamics:** Fabricate and mount transit-style outer body panels, access hatches, and structural sealing to protect against moisture, dust, and vibration.
- **Thermal & Airflow Routing:** Integrate roofline and under-floor louvers designed to align with pre-fabricated compute cassette cooling pathways.
- **Deliverable:** A finished, deployment-ready inner-city rail pod shell designed to receive the drop-in electronics cassette.
