# Student Safety & Operational Protocol Briefing

**Program:** Portsmouth Advanced Career Education (PACE) — Infrastructure & Hardware Engineering Track  
**Document Type:** Mandatory Safety Acknowledgment & Operating Agreement

## Purpose & Scope

This document outlines the mandatory safety protocols, physical security standards, and behavioral guidelines required for all students participating in the fabrication, wiring, and field deployment of modular infrastructure hardware (including rail-mounted compute carts and diagnostic testing rigs).

Working with structural metals, high-density electrical power systems, and industrial right-of-way environments demands strict adherence to safety protocols to protect yourself, your peers, and the equipment.

## Section 1: Shop & Fabrication Safety (Mechanical)

When working in the welding and building trades labs to construct structural enclosures and equipment chassis:

- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Safety glasses with side shields must be worn at all times in the shop area. Approved welding helmets, leather gloves, and heavy cotton/flame-retardant clothing are required during cutting, grinding, and welding operations.
- **Shop Apparel:** No loose clothing, frayed sleeves, or dangling jewelry. Long hair must be tied back securely. Closed-toe leather boots or steel-toe shoes are mandatory.
- **Tool Inspection:** Inspect all hand and power tools for damaged cords, cracked housings, or loose components before use. Report any defects immediately to the instructor. Never bypass safety guards on machinery.
- **Workspace Organization:** Keep walking paths, exits, and work benches clear of scrap metal, extension cords, and tripping hazards. Clean up debris and slag immediately upon completing a task.

## Section 2: Electrical & Zero-Trust Hardware Safety

When assembling internal power racks, routing low-voltage DC lines, and configuring diagnostic ports:

- **Power Isolation:** Always disconnect power sources (batteries, DC power supplies, step-up converters) before wiring, soldering, or modifying circuit components.
- **Circuit Verification:** Double-check all polarity and voltage ratings (for example, 48V-57V DC distribution lines) using a multimeter before energizing any system.
- **Hardwired Security Standards:** Adhere strictly to zero-trust architecture guidelines. Do not install unauthorized consumer wireless modules (such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, or NFC transceivers) into secure hardware test rigs. All telemetry and data connections must utilize designated hardwired pathways (for example, serial or CAN-bus ports).
- **Thermal Awareness:** Be mindful of component heat dissipation under continuous load. Verify proper clearance and insulation to prevent short circuits or thermal strain.

## Section 3: Fieldwork & Right-of-Way (ROW) Safety

When conducting supervised field observations, environmental sensing, or site assessments along designated corridors:

- **Strictly Enforced Boundaries:** Remain only within authorized, designated assessment zones and non-active corridors specified by your instructor. Never trespass on active mainlines or unauthorized private rail property.
- **High-Visibility Gear:** High-visibility safety vests must be worn at all times when outside or near transit environments.
- **Situational Awareness:** Earphones, headphones, and mobile device distractions are strictly prohibited during field observation. Maintain continuous line-of-sight awareness of your surroundings, uneven terrain, and ballast hazards.
- **Emergency Protocols:** In the event of any hazard, unexpected equipment shift, or security concern, immediately alert your instructor and follow evacuation or regrouping instructions.
